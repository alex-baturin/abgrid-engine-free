Version 1.0.0 FREE — September 26, 2026

New Edition

• Released ABGrid Engine FREE, the free edition of ABGrid Engine.
• FREE uses the same basic Grid configuration format as Full.
• Master–Detail in FREE supports one immediate detail grid with depth 1.
• Summary values in FREE are calculated for the current page.
• Sorting in FREE is limited to a single column.
• FREE does not include Subgrid, TreeGrid, DetailsPanels, file uploads, or other advanced Full features.
• Public toast/dialog UI mechanisms and binding autocomplete to arbitrary external inputs are reserved for Full; their internal use by Grid/EditForm remains available.
• FREE has no artificial row or column limits and contains no watermark.
